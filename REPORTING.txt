Paper reporting requirements (2026-10-28)

Keep pretrained and adapted evidence
-----------------------------------
Preserve the complete pretrained benchmark, pilot, raw outputs, and historical
drafts. Fine-tuned rows never replace or overwrite pretrained statistics.
Report both conditions independently and their paired difference on identical
held-out subjects, with explicit model, input, budget, checkpoint and split.
The original full evaluation (6,367 assigned bodies) is distinct from the
adaptation split (987 train / 244 validation / 6,306 protected test bodies).
The adaptation's primary comparison uses the frozen 450 pilot test subjects.
Do not subtract full-benchmark averages from the smaller adaptation-test means
and call that an improvement. Keep the later controlled study separate too.

For each condition, retain expected/scored/valid counts and exclusions; native
and independently normalized geometry scores; failure-aware means and medians;
conditional-valid distances, normals and crease metrics with denominators;
source-weighted summaries; complexity bins; feature/class associations where
supported; tail sensitivity; and actually recorded timing/compute evidence.
Then report paired gains, regressions, shared/changed failures and uncertainty.
Use identical shared eligibility for a paired estimate while separately
reporting all assigned outcomes. Bootstrap the scientific sampling unit
(source family), paired across conditions; label exploratory analyses.
Unavailable records are missing evidence, never fabricated zero results.
Invalid model outputs are retained as benchmark outcomes under the protocol.

Validation chooses a checkpoint; it is not test evidence
-------------------------------------------------------
Keep epoch-zero and every validation/checkpoint history. Do not select epochs,
acceptance gates, prompts, or hyperparameters using test scores.
Report selection-validation changes separately from held-out improvement.
Keep the source-disjoint split and test-subject identifiers/hash intact.
Where only aggregate validation scores are available, do not invent uncertainty,
paired subject outcomes, or eligibility counts. Report those as unavailable.
Geometry-verified self-training is not original design-history supervision.

Caption contract for every table and figure
-------------------------------------------
State what is measured, why it matters to the scientific argument or an
unresolved evidence gap, and the main supported takeaway. Prefer a concise
finding-led caption over a list of visual elements. Include the relevant cohort,
denominator, conditioning, input/compute difference or other limit needed to
interpret it. Keep methods detail in the surrounding text where possible.
Do not imply whole-release coverage, causality, functional semantics, human
annotation validation or held-out adaptation gains that were not measured.
Apply this contract to future autogenerated tables/figures as well.
